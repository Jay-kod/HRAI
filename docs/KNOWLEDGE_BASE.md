# Knowledge Base

Risk rules are database records, not hard-coded in controllers or views. Each active rule evaluates one fact and may produce a factor, condition scope, severity, points, and recommendation trigger.

## Baseline rules

- BMI 25–29.9: overweight factor; BMI 30+: obesity factor.
- Systolic 130–139 or diastolic 80–89: elevated blood-pressure factor.
- Systolic 140+ or diastolic 90+: high blood-pressure factor.
- Smoking, heavy alcohol use, low activity, poor diet, poor sleep, high stress, stroke history, and family history may trigger factors.
- A null fact never triggers a rule.

## Recommendation branches

- High risk: targeted actions, clinician referral language, and short reassessment guidance.
- Moderate risk: triggered actions, a reassessment reminder, and limited general wellness tips.
- Low risk: general activity, diet, sleep, and prevention tips.

Recommendations are educational and must not claim to diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional care.
